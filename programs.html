Programs

Money skills for every stage of growing up

From identifying coins to planning for life after high school, Kash Kids helps students practice money skills at each stage of growing up. Our programs combine age-appropriate instruction with activities that make financial decisions concrete.

Elementary

Elementary financial literacy

Students explore money through books, games, and activities covering coin recognition, earning, needs and wants, saving, spending, and simple budgeting.

Try a sample: Needs, Wants, and a Money Plan, a five-minute interactive lesson for grades 2–5.

Middle & High School

Middle and high school financial literacy

Students practice budgeting, saving and investing, understanding interest, credit and debt, and making decisions about jobs, cars, housing, and other adult expenses.

  • Ongoing classroom instruction throughout the school year
  • 30–90 minute modules on focused topics
  • Entrepreneurship and workforce readiness experiences where available
