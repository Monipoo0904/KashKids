Our mission

Master money skills early

Our mission is to teach K–12 students to master money skills early so they can lead struggle-free lives.

Our story

Helping children understand money before financial challenges shape their choices

Founded by children's author and financial educator Twyla Prindle in Jacksonville, Florida, Kash Kids grew from a commitment to helping children understand money before financial challenges shape their choices. Through books, storytelling, games, and practical activities, we make money concepts approachable and relevant to students' lives.

Our work has grown through relationships with schools, youth-serving organizations, and community partners. Today, Kash Kids combines ongoing classroom instruction with experiences that connect financial literacy to entrepreneurship, workforce readiness, and everyday decisions. Students practice skills they can carry from their first spending choices into their first jobs and adult responsibilities.

We believe students need opportunities to apply what they learn. Our approach gives them repeated practice with money decisions and brings families and community partners into the learning process.
