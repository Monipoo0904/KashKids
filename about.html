Our story

Changing futures, one lesson at a time

Founded in 2014, Kash Kids exists because financial literacy still isn't taught in most classrooms — and the cost of that gap falls hardest on kids who can least afford it.

The mission

Every kid deserves to understand money — before it's a crisis

We believe financial confidence shouldn't depend on which household you grew up in. Kash Kids partners with schools, community centers, and sponsors to deliver hands-on money education directly to K-12 students, completely free to families.

  • Judgment-free, age-appropriate curriculum
  • Delivered in classrooms, after-school, and online
  • Backed by sponsors, volunteers, and partner schools
🎯
Our journey

How we got here

2014

Kash Kids is founded

Founder & Executive Director Twyla Prindle launches Kash Kids with a single after-school workshop, driven by a simple idea: money skills should be taught like any other subject.

2017

First school partnerships

The "Money Makers In Training" curriculum is built out and adopted by local schools, reaching hundreds of students each semester.

2020

Going virtual

Kash Kids launches online lessons and virtual workshops, keeping financial education accessible through remote learning.

Today

3,000+ students a year

Kash Kids now partners with 20+ schools and community organizations, blending in-person events, interactive online lessons, and AR/VR experiences.
