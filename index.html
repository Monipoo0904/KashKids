Why it matters

Money skills should be part of growing up

Most kids graduate having never written a budget or opened a savings account. We close that gap with programs that are fun, practical, and built for how kids actually learn.

Real curriculum

Grade-leveled lessons on saving, spending, credit, debt, and investing — built with educators, not jargon.

Hands-on & fun

Storytelling, games, and AR/VR experiences turn abstract money concepts into something kids remember.

Community powered

Delivered free to students through school partnerships, sponsors, and volunteers who believe every kid deserves this.
