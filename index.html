Working alongside schools

Money skills should be part of growing up

We partner with teachers and schools to give students repeated, hands-on practice with money decisions, building on the learning already happening in their classrooms.

📅

Learning all year

Weekly instruction gives students time to practice and build on earlier lessons.

🤝

Consistent educators

Ongoing classroom relationships help us understand students and support their progress.

💵

Real money experiences

Students in grades 3–12 can earn classroom currency and exchange it for real money through our incentive structure.

🎲

Practical learning

Stories, games, activities, and community experiences connect financial concepts to everyday choices.
